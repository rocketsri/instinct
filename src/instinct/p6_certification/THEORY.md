# P6 theory map

- Question/hypothesis: frozen sections 9.1–9.2.
- Target: simultaneous upper processes over declared computation exits and prefixes; execute the longest prefix with `U <= alpha_t`.
- Assumptions: Bernoulli/bounded prefix-risk event, fixed finite candidate family, declared look schedule, predictable episode spending. Safety risk `alpha_t` and confidence failure `delta_t` are distinct.
- Validity: one-sided exact binomial bounds receive summable confidence spending over looks and Bonferroni weights over prefixes. Episode safety/confidence spends are separately summable.
- Controls: adversarial peeking, prefix selection, fixed-time negative control, exact truth hidden from the method, abstain/execute/union baselines.
- Verdict: validity and nonvacuity are separate. This conservative construction cannot claim improvement over a union bound; P6 remains NARROW until direct AVCRC/CSA/nested-exit baselines are added.
- P6.2 extension: a rollout model is fitted only on pilot units. Held-out cells report model-relative and true-environment coverage/false certification separately. The shift-aware method adds a fixed-time, paired-discordance Clopper--Pearson envelope to a model-relative simultaneous process, splitting confidence error between the two components. Exact model and true risks are evaluation-only.
- P6.2 assumptions: calibration and evaluation share the same paired-discordance law; the declared prefix family and exits are fixed; calibration pairs, model rollouts, and execution outcomes are disjoint; failures are Bernoulli and nested. Violation of exchangeability or an unrepresented shift invalidates the true-environment claim.
- P6.2 controls: unadjusted model certificate, fixed-time pointwise, declared-exit union, abstain/execute, a Bernoulli AVCRC-style growing-sample analogue, and a threshold-only CSA-style e-process analogue. The analogues are bounded mechanism controls, not reproductions of the general conformal methods.
- P6.2 verdict: STOP deployment if no method is valid and nonvacuous under shift; otherwise remain NARROW until the paired-shift assumption and non-Bernoulli learned dynamics are tested. GO still requires material utility over the conservative union comparator.
- P6.3 frontier: preserve P6.2 and evaluate a fresh 16-prefix, 8,192-trajectory, 8,192-exit CPU family. The primary method composes an independent paired-discordance shift envelope with a finite-grid CSA Bernoulli likelihood-ratio e-process. For adjusted null `q` and predictable alternative `theta < q`, each factor has expectation at most one for every Bernoulli risk `p >= q`; Ville validity plus prefix Bonferroni supports max-certified selection without an exit-count penalty.
- P6.3 AVCRC control: implement Theorem 4.1's stitched bounded-loss correction on the model-risk family, with `B=1`, and subtract the largest simultaneous shift radius to retain monotonicity. This is not the paper's importance-weighted shift theorem because density ratios are unavailable. The common-radius composition can be vacuous when only late prefixes shift sharply.
- P6.3 verdict: at the largest scale, require true false-certification upper confidence at most 0.05 and nonvacuity in every family, at least 0.3 mean selected actions over the robust finite union, and at least 10x certificate-only latency speedup. Shift-calibration latency is measured separately and remains part of end-to-end cost.
- P6.4 learned dynamics: fit action-conditioned three-state transition matrices from pilot trajectories, certify only sampled rollouts from the fitted model, and evaluate stable, recovery-loss, late-hazard, and entry-rate structural shifts on fresh units. Dynamic-programming risks for the fitted and true transition systems remain evaluation-only.
- P6.4 scale-readiness: retain the independent paired-discordance envelope and CSA/union/AVCRC boundaries. Report selected model and true risk separately, plus fitting, rollout, calibration, certificate latency, transition count, and measured array working sets. The bounded gate requires true validity, nonvacuity, and a locked 0.2-action gain over robust union; failure of only the gain remains NARROW.
- Source: proposal version 2.1; portfolio amendment 2.1 changes scheduling only.
