# Portfolio execution amendment 2.1

**Date:** 2026-08-16  
**Scope:** portfolio scheduling only; no proposal estimand, objective, baseline, or verdict threshold changes.

Version 2.0 limited Stage D to at most two paper-scale directions. That cap is removed. Every proposal reaching GO or useful NARROW may continue while it satisfies its own scientific gates and compute ceiling. GPU jobs remain serialized by available T4 capacity; CPU experiments, reviews, and theory work may continue independently.

This amendment does not retroactively change any result and does not authorize a failed or unidentifiable proposal to scale. Selection remains evidence-based per Stage D's four criteria.
