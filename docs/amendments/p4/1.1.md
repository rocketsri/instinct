# P4 amendment 1.1 — bounded verifier branch

## Why this branch is recorded separately

The original P4.2 and recovery 1 did not show a meaningful benefit from the
specified prefix-slack training mechanism. Recovery 2 therefore tested a
distinct inference-time use of the frozen recovery estimate. Its first fresh
unit produced a `0.050642` goal-probability gain, but missed the preregistered
expected-call window (`3.698005` versus `4.00 +/- 0.25`). That STOP remains
preserved. The mismatch is a cost-estimation/instrumentation failure (F2), not
evidence for or against the measured recovery-gate effect.

## Versioned branch and unchanged original claim

This amendment does **not** replace

\[
L=L_{task}+\beta\sum_j[m-R_j(s)]_+
\]

or claim that an inference-time gate establishes prefix recovery-slack
training. It permits one F2 repair of recovery 2 on untouched evaluation units:
the gate receives exactly two intervention calls on every 12-step trajectory,
in addition to two long-chunk planning calls. It spends calls when the frozen
pilot threshold fires and uses a deadline rule to spend any remainder. The
threshold, estimator, task/recovery policies, success threshold, and four-call
budget are frozen. Only the accounting defect and held-out layout change.

The independent P4 v11 frontier audit classifies any positive result as NARROW
evidence for inference-time task/recovery switching, not a rescue of P4's
training novelty. It also identifies the existing stage-two expectation/quantile
mismatch as a blocker for every future slack-training claim.

## Falsification and next gate

The verifier branch is retained only if the fresh result improves goal
probability by at least `0.01` at exactly four calls. Failure archives bounded
P4.2 after both scientific recovery cycles. Passing unlocks only a fuller
matched-resource verifier comparison: scorer queries, observations, latency,
policy queries, random/periodic controls, inactivity, and clean-task loss.
